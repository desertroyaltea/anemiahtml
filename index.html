QuickNemia

Point-of-Care Anemia Screening

How to Scan

1

Position the Patient

Ask patient to look up. Gently pull down the lower eyelid to expose the conjunctiva.

2

Good Lighting

Ensure adequate natural or artificial lighting. Avoid harsh shadows or direct glare.

3

Capture Image

Hold camera 10-15cm away. Focus on the lower eyelid conjunctiva. Capture or upload image.

4

Review Results

AI will analyze the image and provide Hb estimation with anemia status. Heat map shows AI focus areas.

Note: This tool is for screening purposes only. Clinical correlation and confirmatory laboratory testing are recommended.
